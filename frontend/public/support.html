Getting Started

Cellami requires a companion desktop application to function. Make sure you have:

  1. Downloaded the Cellami app for Mac or Windows
  2. Installed and launched the app (look for it in your system tray or dock)
  3. Installed the Excel add-in from Microsoft AppSource

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I see "Connection Failed"?

Make sure the Cellami desktop app is running. If using Chrome or Edge, you may need to adjust browser security settings - the add-in will guide you through this.

Is my data sent to the cloud?

No. By default, all AI processing happens locally on your machine. Your Excel data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly configure a third-party API.

Which AI models are supported?

Cellami works with Ollama and supports any model you have installed locally, including Llama, Mistral, Qwen, and more.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes! The desktop companion app is available for both macOS and Windows.
