Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Data Collection

Cellami ("the Add-in") connects to a companion application running locally on your device. All AI processing is performed locally on your machine. The Add-in does not transmit your Excel data to external cloud servers owned by the publisher.

2. Third-Party AI Providers

If you choose to enable optional cloud-based features (such as external API connections), your data may be transmitted to the configured third-party provider. The publisher does not have access to this data, and its handling is subject to that provider's privacy policy.

3. Local Data Storage

Data stored by the Cellami desktop application (such as Knowledge Base files and Chat History) is persisted only on your local hard drive. You maintain full control and ownership of this data.

4. Telemetry

The Add-in may collect anonymous technical telemetry (such as crash reports) to improve stability. This telemetry does not contain personal information or user content.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please visit our support page.
